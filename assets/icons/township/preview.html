Vernacular Sign-Writing Design System

Every icon in this suite was built from direct morphological study of the three uploaded references:

1. King Tiger Hair Clinique (Ref 1)

Provided the dusty-rose plaster wall palette, hand-painted cerulean drop shadows, the 5-point royal crown motif, and the soulful, expressive facial detailing with almond eyes and white iris catchlights.

2. T-Bone Hair Cut (Ref 2)

Provided the corrugated zinc sheet texture, dimensional 3D lettering style with dual-color shadow depth, and the iconic South African street portraiture with styled fade and patterned red collar.

3. African Hair Salon (Ref 3)

Provided the graphic white gouache curved reflection streaks seen on the painted cosmetic bottles, rich chrome yellows with cobalt blue caps/labels, and dignified South African pride.
